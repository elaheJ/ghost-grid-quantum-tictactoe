How to play

This version follows Allan Goff's superposition rule: spooky marks may share a square. A square holds at most two marks. Placement continues until every square holds at least one mark, and then the pairs are measured.

Place: X and O take turns. Choose a Classical move, which puts one real mark in a completely empty square, or a Superposition move, which puts a matching pair of spooky marks in two different squares. Each pair has its own color and move number; the first square is A and the second is B. A spooky mark may go into a square that already holds one other spooky mark. A real mark locks its square, so nothing further may be placed there.

Entanglement: two pairs that share a square are entangled, exactly as in Goff's article. They cannot both end up in that square, so collapsing one forces the other into its far square, and that forcing can cascade through a chain of shared squares. In this version the chain pauses after the roll. Each click on Next step resolves one forced collapse, so you can follow the chain square by square.

Measure when every square is filled: once no square is empty, take turns measuring the remaining pairs. Each player may select and measure only their own pairs: X measures X, and O measures O. If the next player has no pairs remaining, measurement passes to the other player. Select your half on the playing pad, then click Roll die. An odd roll keeps A and an even roll keeps B. The surviving half becomes a real mark for the pair's owner. If another pair shares that square, it waits for Next step, which pushes it into its far square. Each push can start the next one. The measurement ends when the chain ends, and the emptied squares become available again.

Continue or finish: after every pair is measured, three real X's or O's in a row wins. Spooky marks never count toward a win. If nobody has won and empty squares remain, the next player continues placing until every square is filled again. Repeat until a win or a draw. A full board of real marks with no winning line is a draw.

Conventions for details Goff leaves open here: odd → A and even → B; placement and measurement share one alternating turn order; if both players hold a winning line once all pairs are measured, the game is a draw. Dice rolls are generated locally and require no internet.

Controls: click the pad or press 1–9. During measurement a shared square selects only the current player's marks; repeated clicks cycle between that player's pairs if there are two. Squares holding only the other player's marks are disabled. During a chain, click Next step or either highlighted square to resolve one step. The Enter key, the right arrow key, and Page Down also work. Escape cancels a pending selection. Undo cancels a selection or reverses the last placement, roll, or chain step. New game starts over. For projection, open this file directly in a browser and click Full screen.
